# Product

## Register

product

## Users
Finance controllers and payment operations teams reviewing a bounded settlement batch across gateway, bank, and merchant-ledger sources. They need to scan exposure, inspect evidence, review abstentions, and verify that the system did not silently book an unsafe match.

## Product Purpose
SettleGraph is an evidence-first reconciliation controller. It turns three disagreeing financial views into traceable decisions, books only invariant-verified matches, and keeps ambiguous or unexplained money visible for human review.

## Brand Personality
Precise, calm, accountable.

## Anti-references
Avoid generic navy SaaS dashboards, decorative card grids, marketing-style hero layouts, AI-generated gradients, excessive rounded controls, and interfaces that make safety claims without showing the underlying evidence or denominator.

## Design Principles
- Evidence should be visually closer than explanation: put the decision state, amount, and provenance in the first scan.
- Safety is a product state, not a marketing claim: abstentions, exceptions, quarantine, and replay status must remain visible.
- Editorial restraint earns trust: use neutral surfaces and one functional accent instead of decoration.
- Density should serve review: tables and controls stay compact, but important financial exposure gets breathing room.
- Every interactive control must communicate its state and remain usable under keyboard and reduced-motion settings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Target WCAG 2.2 AA basics: readable contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic headings and buttons, no color-only status communication, responsive layouts, and reduced-motion support. Financial status colors must always be paired with text labels.
